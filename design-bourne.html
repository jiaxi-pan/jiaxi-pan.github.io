The Bourne Protocol

50 poster iterations inspired by The Bourne Identity, expanded into an interactive decryption game in a Figma prototype.

Year

Play the prototype

About this project

For Graphic Design: 50 Iterations, I redesigned the poster for The Bourne Identity fifty times, then turned the series into an interactive decryption game in Figma. The game carries the film’s suspense into the interface. Any visual detail may hide a clue, and clicking the red stamp opens the complete collection of posters. Players have to look closely and act to move forward, which links this project to my research on how people take part.
