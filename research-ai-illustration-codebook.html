Codebook

The thirteen comment categories used in Audience Engagement with AI Illustration on Xiaohongshu and Instagram.

Download PDF

Rules

  • A comment can count in more than one category.
  • Not counted: replies under a comment, ads for the commenter's own tools or accounts, and irrelevant comments. Good wishes that encourage people to interact were recorded separately as other special comments.

Categories

CategoryLevelDefinitionExample
PraiseAppreciativeGeneral positive responses expressing that they look good or appreciation for sharing, sometimes briefly mentioning aspects like color or composition.
ToolInstrumentalAsking which AI tools were used or discussing AI image-generation and other AI tools.
ProcessInstrumentalAsking how the works were created, requesting tutorials, or asking detailed questions about the creation process.
PromptInstrumentalAsking for the natural-language instructions used to generate similar images, or discussing the prompt.
AI Identification and ConfusionDiscursiveAsking whether the images were AI-generated, showing uncertainty or unawareness about their AI origin, or expressing surprise at what AI can achieve.
Aesthetic and Content DiscussionDiscursiveMore detailed discussion of the style, techniques, or content, including preferences or votes for specific works.Comments on the animation style of Hayao Miyazaki.
Industry, Social, and Emotional DiscussionDiscursiveDiscussions of the industry, broader social issues, or the deeper emotional and psychological effects of the work.Is AI going to replace painters?
Application and SuggestionsInstrumentalSuggestions for improvement or further development, ideas for applying the illustrations in other contexts, or requests for collaboration.
Access RequestInstrumentalAsking for the original image, requesting permission for non-commercial use, or indicating an intention to use the image.Asking for a 4K image or a download, or for use as a wallpaper.
Social SharingInstrumentalTagging friends to share the post and invite them to view or engage with it.Tagging a friend with @.
AI Use (Positive)DiscursiveExpressing approval of or appreciation for the use of AI in the artwork.Thanking the creator for tagging the post as AI.
AI Use (Negative)DiscursiveExpressing criticism of or opposition to the use of AI in the artwork.“There’s just something different about AI-generated images.”
Follow-up CreationParticipatorySharing hand-drawn versions or works inspired by the original artwork, or new images created using similar prompts.A drawing of a lighthouse shared under a lighthouse illustration.
