About

I am Jiaxi (Peggy) Pan, a senior at Boston University completing a BFA in Graphic Design and a BS in Advertising, with a minor in Business Administration and Management. I am applying to graduate programs in communication, media and design for Fall 2027.

My research looks at how creators and audiences decide what counts as authentic when AI is part of the work, comparing Chinese and Western platforms. I work in Chinese and English, and I read Xiaohongshu and Instagram from the inside. I am a research assistant at Lo/Be Lab, a small research practice founded and led by Seth Looper, where I am co-authoring a mixed-methods study of how Chinese international students use AI for self-reflection.

Research interests

Culture and the creator economy. How Chinese and Western platforms shape what creators make and how audiences respond, from wanghong culture to AI-made content.

AI and self-reflection. How people, and international students in particular, use AI as a mirror to think through identity, goals, and a hard transition abroad.

Cross-cultural technology. How tools built on Western assumptions fit, or fail to serve, users from more relational, collectivist backgrounds.

Platform and content analysis. Studying these questions through what people post and say, alongside surveys and interviews.

Exhibition

Tone in Tongue, presented by Radical Characters, July to November 2025. Selected among 30 international artists from 250+ submissions. Shown at Otis College of Art and Design (Bolsky Gallery), Shanghai Printing Technology Research Institute, and Maryland Institute College of Art (Bronze Gallery).
