Class study, Spring 2026

College Students’ Perceptions of AI in Super Bowl Advertisements

Authors
Linda Liu, Jiaxi Pan, Ian Krockel, James McIntyre, Sydney Farrell
Course
CM 321 Communication Research Methods, Boston University

A five-person mixed-methods class study of how college students perceive AI in Super Bowl advertising, combining semi-structured interviews with a survey.

My part

  • Conducted a share of the 20 semi-structured interviews and recruited participants through personal networks.
  • Helped develop the team's qualitative codebook and applied it to interview transcripts.
  • Contributed to writing the final report.
